Privacy Policy

This OAuth application is a personal configuration of rclone, used solely by its owner to access their own Google Drive from their own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • No data is collected, stored, or processed by any server operated for this application. rclone runs locally and talks directly to Google's APIs.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
